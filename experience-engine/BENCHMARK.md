# EMORA benchmark — e-invitation × etaklif Pro × Celamur

Reviewed 2026-09-28.

## What to borrow as product principles (not copy visually)

### e-invitation.uz
- strongest at art direction and tactile editorial materials
- watercolor botanicals, aged parchment, cream paper, blue ink, dark Islamic sky, gold/emerald, black-and-white torn-paper editorial
- invitation remains practical: names, date, venue, map, mobile preview, music
- templates feel intentionally art-directed rather than recolored variants

### etaklifpro.uz
- strongest at format breadth
- opening card, falling flowers, polaroid scrapbook, rose-wall reveal, cloud opening, glass cards, suzani/zardozi, star sky, wax seal, slideshow, cinema/projector, photo gallery, luxe map/card
- strong catalog promise: each design has a recognizable visual mechanism

### celamur.com
- strongest at interaction dramaturgy
- one link = one small ritual
- handwritten name → wax seal fracture → ink appearing live → polaroids on paper → computational finale
- finale is not decoration; it is the emotional payoff
- gesture should cause physical-looking response, with sound/haptic where available
- no meaningless scrolling between beats

## EMORA quality gate

Every experience must include:
1. A unique opening object or world.
2. A tactile gesture with visible physical consequence.
3. A personalized reveal, not generic filler.
4. Real photos/materials integrated into the composition.
5. A transition where the previous state physically becomes the next.
6. A finale that changes the whole scene.
7. Mobile-first 100svh interaction; no document scrolling inside the experience.
8. Sound/haptic only when it supports the gesture.
9. Typography and material language unique to the template.
10. No generic card UI inside the emotional flow.
