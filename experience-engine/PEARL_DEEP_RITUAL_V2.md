# Pearl Linen · Deep Ritual V2

## Why this pass exists

Pearl V1 had a strong opening but compressed the emotional arc too aggressively.

Public Celamur product language describes its core experience as roughly **one minute from envelope to finale**, with:
- handwritten recipient name
- wax seal fracture
- paragraph-by-paragraph ink reveal
- up to three polaroids placed inside the letter
- a high-effort final portrait
- save/share/response after the payoff

Pearl V2 adopts the dramaturgy principle, not Celamur's artwork or implementation.

## Target rhythm

Target recipient pace: **60–75 seconds**, depending on reading speed and interaction.

### 00 · Arrival — 0–8s
The linen desk breathes.
Recipient name writes itself.
The envelope waits.
No instruction until the composition settles.

### 01 · Seal — 8–16s
Envelope comes forward.
Recipient holds the wax seal.
520ms pressure → fracture → haptic/crack.
Short silence after impact.

### 02 · Paper rise — 16–22s
The same paper physically exits the envelope.
No scene cut.
Envelope remains underneath long enough to preserve material continuity.

### 03 · Live ink — 22–38s
Only paragraph one appears.
Recipient chooses when to continue.
Paragraph two appears through the same paper fibers.
Recipient chooses again.
Paragraph three arrives last.
The experience waits for reading instead of racing ahead.

### 04 · Memories — 38–55s
Only the first polaroid drops.
Touch/drag it to unlock the second.
Second unlocks the third.
Each arrival has its own physical impact.
The desk gradually becomes a memory composition.

### 05 · False ending — 55–64s
After the third memory, everything gathers back toward the letter.
A clean back-side sheet appears:
“Yana bitta narsa bor.”

One pearl sits in the middle.
The experience becomes quiet again.

### 06 · Final hold — 64–68s
Recipient holds the pearl for 900ms.
The pearl fills with wine-colored light.
Releasing early cancels the transition.

### 07 · Visible effort finale — 68–75s
Paper world falls away.
Particles count upward while the portrait/heart form is constructed.
The counter shows the real rendered particle count:
“___ marvarid nuqta · bitta xotira”.

Then the final sentence writes in.
Share / keepsake / replay remain after the emotional payoff.

## Three-gesture budget

1. Hold seal
2. Touch/drag memories
3. Hold final pearl

No extra gesture is allowed unless one of these is removed.

## What must not happen

- no unrelated screen carousel
- no generic cards
- no automatic paragraph dump
- no three polaroids appearing simultaneously
- no random particle transition before the story earns it
- no fake “sent” response without backend
- no public-link claim for local-only media

## Success criteria

Pearl V2 is review-ready only when:
- mobile browser QA passes the full ritual
- no scroll or browser error occurs
- progressive reading works
- memory unlock order works
- early release on pearl cancels
- 900ms hold reaches finale
- visible particle counter is real, not decorative
- opening, letter, memory, afterword, finale screenshots each feel visually distinct
- latest Railway deployment is terminal SUCCESS
