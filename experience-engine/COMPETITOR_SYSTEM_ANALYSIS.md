## e-invitation

### Catalog system

The public catalog currently shows strong design identities instead of one recolored layout:

| Design | Publicly described identity | System lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | falling petals, blush/sage, romantic spring | ambient motion may be enough when art direction is strong |
| Islamic | living stars, dark canvas, Islamic geometry, gold/emerald | environment + cultural ornament can carry the experience |
| Milliy Romance | animated national bride/groom, atlas/adras, suzani florals | culturally specific illustration is a product differentiator |
| Manor | botanical watercolor, aged parchment, poetry-book scrolling | scroll can be correct when the metaphor is editorial |
| Botanical Paper | white paper, green botanicals, handwritten mood | material restraint can still be premium |
| Osh | ivory/gold, all essentials on one screen | premium can be static/minimal |
| Amru Maruf | deep green/gold, Bismillah, verse, countdown | functional data can live inside a formal cultural composition |
| Xatna | navy/gold, royal night mood | ceremony-specific art direction matters |

### Product system

Publicly described capabilities include:
- live preview
- mobile and desktop responsive output
- live animation and music
- permanent share link
- personalised guest greeting
- named guest links
- edit details before payment
- create/preview first, publish after payment
- custom/bespoke design path
- Love Story animation product where sequence, style and duration are agreed around the couple story

### Strongest principle

**The material determines the interaction model.**

Manor scrolls like a poetry book; Osh intentionally remains one-screen; Islamic behaves like a living night composition.

## Celamur

### Experience system

The public product describes a short emotional ritual rather than a conventional invitation page:

- recipient name is handwritten
- wax seal fractures instead of simply opening
- tactile/haptic-like feedback is part of the seal moment
- message appears paragraph by paragraph as live ink
- up to three polaroid photos with handwritten captions sit inside the letter
- final portrait is assembled from thousands of hearts or repeated names
- galaxy can be manipulated by finger before words collapse into a portrait
- final output can be saved/shared

### Product capabilities

Publicly described:
- custom first-screen words
- multiple handwriting choices
- optional sections
- response back to creator
- share/save
- portrait from uploaded photo
- Golden Hour / Starry Night time-based sky
- custom music
- opening timer
- word lock
- creator notifications for opens/responses/chosen day
- short recipient links
- Telegram-centric creation/payment flow

### Strongest principle

**One link = one short ritual with visible effort and one memorable payoff.**

The experience is not made premium by adding many screens. Depth comes from continuity, tactility and the final transformation.

## Gaps EMORA can exploit

### 1. One system that supports multiple experience mediums
Competitors demonstrate different strengths, but EMORA can unify:
- ritual
- world
- editorial
- cinematic
- formal
- atmospheric

under one manifest/editor/publishing system.

### 2. Template-specific editor instead of generic form
Only expose fields the experience can use.

Example:
- Galaxy asks for portrait + messages + music
- Silk asks for couple/event/map + optional photos
- Quiet Room asks for one message + optional sound
- Cinema asks for media timeline + music + final question

### 3. Strong preview before purchase
The creator should see a real recipient-mode preview, not a thumbnail.

### 4. Remixes without destroying identity
Allow:
- language
- content
- photo
- music
- guest greeting
- selected material variant

Do not allow arbitrary settings that break the art direction.

### 5. Measurable experience quality
Separate:
- build correctness
- performance
- accessibility
- visual approval
- emotional/signature-moment approval

A CI green check is not visual sign-off.
